Appreciate all of the buildings and exhibits you are interested in, fill up at any of the restaurants, and peruse the shops for the perfect gifts and memorabilia.
Because the park is expansive, we recommend planning your route in advance in order to make the most of your experience.

Assigned Entrance Times (only for Ghibli's Grand Warehouse)
9 a.m. (*) / 10 a.m. / 11 a.m. / 12 p.m. / 1 p.m. / 2 p.m.
For this pass, Ghibli's Grand Warehouse is the only area with assigned entry times.
Please enter Ghibli's Grand Warehouse no later than one hour after your reservation time. Early entry is not allowed.
Four areas other than Ghibli's Grand Warehouse do not have specified entry times. Please enter between opening time and 4:30 p.m. (Final entry to all four areas is at 4:30 p.m.) You are required to queue when other visitors are waiting. Especially, Hill of Youth and Dondoko Forest are expected to be very crowded after 4:00 p.m. until closing time (5:00 p.m.). We recommend visiting these areas in the morning or early afternoon.
▼Notice about Wristbands
When your Ghibli Park O-Sanpo Day Pass Premium ticket (QR code) is checked by staff at the first area you visit, you will receive a wristband. After that, please show your wristband at the entrance of each area you visit. When you enter your first area of the day, all members of your party must be present and must enter with the person whose name is on the ticket.
In addition, when entering Ghibli's Grand Warehouse, all members of your party must be present and must enter with the person whose name is on the ticket. At your designated entrance time, please present your ticket (QR code) instead of your wristband.
With the exception of Ghibli's Grand Warehouse and the first area you visit, all other areas can be entered individually by presenting your wristband.
Re-entry to each area is possible. (Re-entry to Ghibli's Grand Warehouse is available after 3:00 p.m. only.)
Wristband pickup must be completed by 12:00 p.m. on the day of your visit.
Ghibli Park O-Sanpo Day Pass Standard
This pass goes on sale two months in advance, on the 10th at 2 p.m. (Japan time)
You can enter Ghibli's Grand Warehouse, Mononoke Village and Valley of Witches with this pass.
You will have access to the cafés and restaurants, shops, play equipment and rides. (some activities require additional fees)
Only Ghibli's Grand Warehouse has an assigned entrance time. Please select an entrance time when purchasing your tickets.
If you don't know where to start, this pass is the one for you.
Ghibli's Grand Warehouse is bursting with all things Studio Ghibli as far as the eye can see.
The buildings and cityscapes throughout Valley of Witches are inspired by the films featuring witches.
In Mononoke Village, Tatara-ba and other structures blend right into the wide-open park atmosphere. You can feel the transitions from season to season here.
While experiencing the world of Studio Ghibli, you can take commemorative photos and buy memorabilia. The day will be over before you know it.

Same-day Tickets for Vally of Witches Designated Buildings
Same-day tickets are required to enter Okino Residence, Howl's Castle and The House of Witches. (Ghibli Park O-Sanpo Day Pass Premium holders are not required to purchase additional tickets for entry into the designated buildings.)
At 10:00 a.m. (9:00 a.m. on weekends and holidays), Exchange Vouchers which indicate a specific sale time will be distributed for each building near the Ticket Truck in Valley of Witches.
The Exchange Voucher can be used to purchase the Same-day Ticket of each building. You can purchase the Same-day Ticket from the time indicated on your voucher until 4:30 p.m. at the Ticket Truck in Valley of Witches.
Exchange vouchers with the earliest sales times are distributed first, on a first-come first-served basis.
Please note that the number of tickets is limited, and sales will end once tickets are sold out. Thank you for your understanding.
Sales may be temporarily suspended due to crowding.
*The Same day Tickets are only available to those holding a valid ticket for Valley of Witches on the same date.
*In order to avoid crowd related accidents, please remain calm as you move throughout the park.

Terms and Conditions
*Entry times listed with (※) above are only available on days when operating hours start at 9 a.m.
*Advanced reservations are required for entry weekdays, weekends and holidays with no exceptions.
*When visiting areas that have no assigned entry time, you may enter whenever you like before 4:30 p.m. although you will be required to queue when other visitors are waiting.
*We may limit entry to each area, in the event of overcrowding or other issues.
*Separate fees are required for the following activities and rides:
Interactive workshops at Tatara-ba (Mononoke Village)
Carousel (Valley of Witches)
Flying Machine (Valley of Witches)
*Some facilities may not be available for use in the event of rain. These include:
Lord Okkoto (Mononoke Village), the walking trail in Dondoko Forest, Carousel and Flying Machine (Valley of Witches)
*Some facilities and activities are limited to children of 12 years and under including the following:
Cat Bus Room, Children's Town (Ghibli's Grand Warehouse), and Dondoko-do (Dondoko Forest).
*Half-price tickets are available for visitors holding disability certificate plus one companion. Please present either the original or a photocopy of the disability certificate and passport to the staff for confirmation when entering the park.
*In addition to admission, a service charge will be applied when tickets are issued. (excluding same-day tickets to enter Okino Residence, Howl's Castle and The House of Witches)
*Discounted tickets are not available, including for groups or early booking.
Purchasing Tickets
Tickets for January 2027
On sale Tuesday November 10th 2026 at 2 p.m.
*The dates tickets go on sale and sale periods are subject to change.
*The purchase of each ticket type is limited to once per month, but a maximum of six tickets may be purchased at one time. Children up to the age of three may enter for free and should not be counted as a member in the party.
*Ghibli Park does not offer designated reservations for large groups. If you wish to visit the park as a party of 7 or more, you will need to divide your party into "small groups" of 6 or less when making the ticket reservations. Each "small group" of 6 or less will need a designated applicant to register their information. All members of each "small group" must enter each area at the same time as the designated applicant whose name is on the tickets, so please make sure all members of each "small group" are present when you enter.
*Please note that park tickets cannot be purchased at the entrance of each area. (excluding same-day tickets sold in Valley of Witches to enter Okino Residence, Howl's Castle and The House of Witches)
*In order to prevent ticket resales, you may be randomly selected to show identification upon entering. The person whose name is on the tickets must be present and able to provide ID. Please bring your passport when you visit the park.
Purchasing tickets on behalf of visitors is prohibited.
*The purchase of tickets for resale and/or transfer purposes (including gifts) is strictly prohibited.
*The purchase of tickets for the purpose of reselling may be punishable by law and is strictly prohibited.
*Visitors whose tickets appear to have been purchased through auction (or other unofficial means) will be denied entry.
*After a booking has been made, no refunds or changes to dates, times, ticket types, or the number of tickets are accepted.
*Once a purchase has been made, tickets will not be reissued even if they are lost, damaged or stolen.
*All members of your party must enter each area at the same time as the person whose name is on the tickets, so please make sure all members of your party are present when you enter. (Electronic tickets cannot be distributed to each person.)
For those with Ghibli Park O-Sanpo Day Pass Premium, all members must be present before entering the first area and Ghibli's Grand Warehouse, but other areas can be entered individually by presenting your wristbands.
*Before purchasing your tickets, please read the FAQ and COVID-19 prevention sections on this website.
