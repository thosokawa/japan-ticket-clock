How to purchase tickets
Purchasing tickets online
Select the date, destination and number of passengers
1. After selecting the date choose the destination.
*For down-train select the Saga / Arashiyama to Kameoka, for up-train select Kameoka to Saga / Arashiyama.
2. Enter the number of passengers, up to a total of 8 people.
3. Press "Proceed to reservation" to continue.
Select the boarding and disembarking stations, train car and seat.
1. After selecting the boarding and disembarking stations, select the train you wish to board from the trains displayed.
2. After selecting the train car, a seat map will be displayed, so please select your seat.
*Grayed-out seats can not be selected.
3. Select the number of seats for the number of passengers, press "Next", confirm the selected seat information, then press "Next" to proceed.
Create new account
If this is your first time using the site, please register a new account. If you already have an account please log in.
You can register with either your social media account or e-mail address.
After confirming the booking details proceed to payment
1. Verify the reservation and payment details.
Your name will be displayed as your account name. If you wish to change it, please write the new name into the appropriate field.
2. Read the confirmation details and conditions, tick off the "Agree" checkbox and press "Confirm Payment" to proceed.
3. After entering your credit card information, press "Pay" to complete your purchase.
*Reservation information will be sent to your registered e-mail address.
How to use tickets purchased online
Display the ticket from your account page
https://ars-saganokanko.triplabo.jp/home
Log in through the URL above, open the My Page menu and view the list of reservations.
Press 'View Details' to view your reservation information, then press 'Redeem E-Ticket' to proceed.
Select the ticket and display the QR code
After selecting the ticket to be used, present the QR code to the attendant at the ticket gate and board the train in your designated seat.
How to purchase tickets at the ticket office
Individual customers
Same-day tickets are sold on a first-come, first-served basis at each Sagano Romantic Train station counter(except Torokko Hozukyo station).
Same-day ticket sales start time
Torokko Saga Station, around 8:35 a.m.
Torokko Arashiyama Station: around 8:50 a.m.
Torokko Kameoka Station: around 9:10 a.m.
*A limited number of standing tickets will be sold after reserved seats are sold out.
Groups
Same day tickets are exempt from group discounts.
