Gion Matsuri’s Yamahoko Junko (Float Procession), one of Japan’s three biggest festivals; and arguably its most extravagant, is set to take place on July 17 and 24.
Its paid viewing seat tickets have went on sale at 10:00 AM JST, June 1 (Mon), 2026
Reserve one of the paid viewing seats to enjoy watching it in a more engaging and comfortable enviornment.
The following seats are available for purchase this year:
・ General seats
・ Special learning seats , which include live commentary in English or Japanese (available only on July 24)
・ Premium seats , which include live commentary in English or Japanese, as well as beverages, access to a buffet, and more!
Come enjoy the spectacular Gion Matsuri Festival with us!
*A portion of the proceeds from the sales of the tickets will be used to preserve and continue the festival.
