What you can do with JR-EAST Train Reservation
1
Rail Pass Purchase
Hokkaido Rail Pass
Sapporo-Noboribetsu Area Pass
Sapporo-Furano Area Pass
JR East-South Hokkaido Rail Pass
JR Tohoku-South Hokkaido Rail Pass
Purchase available from
10 am (JST) one month before the starting date of the rail pass
2
Seat Reservation
(using a rail pass)
If you purchase a rail pass with JR-EAST Train Reservation
Reservation available from
10 am (JST) one month before the boarding date until 6 minutes before departure time
If you purchase an exchange order for a rail pass at travel agencies
Reservation available from
10 am (JST) one month before the boarding date until 11:50 pm (JST) on the two days before the boarding date
*You must receive tickets by 9 pm (JST) on the day before the boarding date
3
Ticket Purchase
(without a rail pass) (Reserved and non-reserved seats)
Purchase available from
10 am (JST) one month before the boarding date until 6 minutes before departure time
*E-tickets are available for purchase until the train's scheduled departure time.
*A basic fare ticket is required to take any trains.
(IC cards can also be used as a basic fare ticket if it’s inside the IC card usable area.)
If you don’t have one, please purchase one when selecting a train you will take.
Trains that you can reserve
(only for JR Hokkaido area)
Hokkaido Shinkansen
Limited express trains (Hokuto, Suzuran, Ozora, Tokachi, Lilac, Kamui, Okhotsk, Soya, Sarobetsu)
★There are e-tickets for reserved seats on the Special Rapid Airport, Rapid Airport, and Semi-Rapid Airport trains.
(A basic fare ticket or IC card is required separately)
*You cannot reserve seats for temporary trains, sightseeing trains and local trains.
e.g.) AKAIHOSHI, AOIHOSHI, SL Fuyu-no-Shitsugen Train, Ryuhyo Monogatari Train etc.
